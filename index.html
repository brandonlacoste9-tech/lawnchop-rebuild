FAQ

Questions, answered.

How often will my driveway get cleared?

Our team runs the snow route whenever accumulation reaches 2 or more inches — automatically, no need to call.

What times do you come by?

Between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM, per local noise bylaws — and late enough in the morning for you to move vehicles out first.

Can I leave my vehicle in the driveway?

Absolutely. Operators keep a minimum 2-foot distance from vehicles and clear the path out to the roadway.

What equipment do you use?

Tractors with snowblowers — efficient clearing with snow safely piled on front lawns.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — one-time seasonal payment (save 10%) or spread-out installments. Senior rates available for clients over 60.

Which areas do you serve?

Fort St. John city limits for snow removal at this time.
